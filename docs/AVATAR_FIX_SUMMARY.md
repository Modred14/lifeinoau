# Avatar fix summary

## Root causes found
1. **Male not masculine (and female flat):** `build_base.py` applied only the `african-<gender>-young` race delta. MakeHuman's real macro model also needs `universal-<gender>-young-<muscle>-<weight>` (shoulders, jaw, chest, hips). Without it both bodies kept the neutral base's shape. Now applied, plus male/female face and body detail targets (jaw, chin, brow, nose, lips, neck, V-torso, limbs).
2. **Hair floating / gaps / jagged shaved hairline:** hair was placed with hard-coded sphere/circle numbers and a cap cut along mesh triangles. Rebuilt against the real scalp (ray-cast from inside the skull), with a smooth per-style hairline curve and a dithered strand-like edge. Everything is skinned 100% to the Head bone (long braids blend into the upper back).
3. **Clothes near-black:** sRGB values were written as linear glTF colours. Fixed. Trousers were wide pipes; now tapered/straight with clearance from the body, smoothed cloth, clean hem/neckline.
4. **Skin:** baked roughness map (oily T-zone, matte cheeks, glossy lips), natural lips, brows with strokes, lash line, under-eye shade, nostrils, male stubble; physical skin material with soft sheen in `loadAvatar.js`; hair edges use alpha-to-coverage.
5. Male scaled to ~1.77 m.

## Tested (Chromium/SwiftShader, the project's own loader and lights)
Front/side/back/three-quarter/face for M lowcut, M afro, F braids, F afro; idle and walk poses; amber and ebony skin tones. No loader errors. Renders in `docs/avatar-renders/`.

## Not done / limits
- No Blender here: models are regenerated by the Python pipeline from MPFB2 (CC0) data, not hand-sculpted. Result is a clear improvement, not photoreal.
- Only the 4 hairstyles and the `campus` outfit exist as GLBs. Other options in `lib/look.js` (bun, locs, owambe, ...) still fall back to the built-in procedural character, as before.
- Only `idle` and `walk` clips exist (run/sit etc. fall back to idle, as before).
- Small thin skin-coloured slits remain at the shoulder/neckline of the clothing on close-ups.
- Full-body sneakers remain simple.
- Rebuild: `python3 tools/avaters/build_base.py <mpfb2>/src/mpfb/data <out> both` then `build_parts.py <data> <out>` (needs numpy, scipy, Pillow, pygltflib).

## Update: all remaining hairstyles and outfits now exist
- Women hair: bun, ponytail, long, locs, lowcut, gele (headwrap), classic (bob). Men hair: locs, braids (cornrows), classic.
- Outfits for both genders: casual, office, owambe, sitework (plus the existing campus).
- All 70 hair x outfit combinations were rendered through the real loader; every one loads as a real model (none fall back to the procedural character).
- Loader: materials named "Accent..." (gele, trim, cuffs) take the player's accent colour.
- Limits: no hard hat for sitework; fabric patterns (ankara/adire/aso-oke) are not applied; only idle and walk animations exist.
